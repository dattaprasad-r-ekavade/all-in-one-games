You do one small task card from `docs/TASKS.md`.

1. Read `AGENTS.md` and your task card.
2. Follow the card exactly. Do not redesign or refactor code that the card does not name.
3. If the task needs a decision that the card and the plan do not make, stop and ask in the handoff note.
4. Run the **Done when** commands.
5. Commit with `<TASK-ID>: <summary>` and end with the handoff note from `AGENTS.md` section 7.
