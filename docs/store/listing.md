## Full description

Give your mind a quiet challenge with Witgrid, a hub of logic and puzzle games for a quick break or a longer solve. Every game works offline, with no account.

Choose a puzzle:

• Sudoku, Mini Sudoku and Killer Sudoku: classic number placement, from a fast 6×6 grid to cage sums.
• Nonogram: reveal a hidden pattern from the row and column clues.
• Solitaire: the classic Klondike card game, draw one, with deals that can be won.
• Colour Sort: pour the liquid until each tube holds one colour, a calm water sort and color sort puzzle.
• Number Link: connect each pair of matching numbers and fill every cell, like a connect-the-dots pipe puzzle.
• Chess Puzzles: find the mate in one, two or three moves.
• Pet Crowns: place one crown in each row, column and region, in the style of a star battle puzzle.
• Towers: a skyscrapers logic puzzle where edge clues count the buildings you can see.
• Binary: a binary puzzle with two symbols in balanced rows and columns.
• Futoshiki: fill the grid with numbers that follow the greater-than and less-than signs.
• Mosaic: fill cells so each number counts the filled cells around it.
• Arrows: tap each arrow with a clear path and slide it off the board.

Play through difficulty packs or try a daily puzzle. Use a hint to see a logical next step, and undo or redo moves. Your progress, best times, daily completions and streak are saved as you play.

Make the game comfortable for you with light or dark themes, colourways, adjustable system text size, and an optional reduce-motion setting. You can also set a local reminder if you want one.

Core puzzle play works offline, and no account is required. An internet connection is used to load ads and to make or restore a Premium purchase.

Witgrid contains ads. Optional rewarded ads can provide a hint, continue, or streak repair. An interstitial may appear after you finish a level and tap Next. Ads never appear while a puzzle is active. The optional one-time Premium purchase removes interstitials and provides unlimited hints, continues, and eligible streak repairs without an ad.
