## Full description

Give your mind a quiet challenge with Witgrid, a collection of four logic games for a quick break or a longer solve.

Choose a puzzle:

• Crowns: place one crown in each row, column, and region.
• Binary: complete each row with a balanced pattern and no run of three matching cells.
• Nonogram: reveal an abstract pattern by following the row and column clues.
• Mini Sudoku: fill a 6×6 grid with digits 1 to 6, following the row, column, and box rules.

Play through difficulty packs or try a daily puzzle. Use a hint to see a logical next step, check your entries, and undo or redo moves. Your progress, best times, daily completions, and streak are saved as you play.

Make the game comfortable for you with light or dark themes, colourways, adjustable system text size, and an optional reduce-motion setting. You can also set a local reminder if you want one.

Core puzzle play works offline, and no account is required. An internet connection is used to load ads and to make or restore a Premium purchase.

Witgrid contains ads. Optional rewarded ads can provide a hint, check, continue, or streak repair. An interstitial may appear after you finish a level and tap Next. Ads never appear while a puzzle is active. The optional one-time Premium purchase removes interstitials and provides unlimited hints, checks, continues, and eligible streak repairs without an ad.
