Witgrid: Offline Logic Puzzles — Privacy Policy

Developer: Datathecodie · Effective date: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app Witgrid: Offline Logic Puzzles ("Witgrid", "the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. Witgrid has no user accounts and no developer server. We do not sell personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device so that your games work, including offline:

  • puzzle progress, saved boards, solve times, best times and statistics;
  • daily puzzle completions and streak information;
  • settings, such as theme, colourway, reduce-motion, timer and reminder preferences;
  • whether you have bought Premium, and your consent choices for ads.

We do not receive this information. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this app data in your device backup, under Google's backup terms. This lets your progress move to a new phone.

2. Advertising

Witgrid shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Ads never appear while a puzzle is active. When the app requests an ad, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID;
  • approximate location, estimated from your IP address;
  • app interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic and performance information.

Google uses this data for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your choice with Google's User Messaging Platform before it requests personalised ads. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android device settings.

3. Purchases

The optional Premium upgrade is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Play terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store your card number, billing address or other payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether you own Premium, and it stores the result on your device.

4. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No information is sent to us for this feature.

5. Analytics and crash reports

This version of Witgrid does not send analytics or crash reports to us. If a future version adds them, they will be off until you turn them on in Settings, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

6. Children

Witgrid is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

Information in section 1 stays on your device until you delete it. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Because we have no server and no accounts, we hold no copy of this information to delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, use your device's ad settings and the controls described in Google's privacy policy.

8. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to check Premium purchases.
  • Notifications: only for the optional daily reminder.
  • Run at start-up: to restore your reminder after the phone restarts.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.

10. Contact
