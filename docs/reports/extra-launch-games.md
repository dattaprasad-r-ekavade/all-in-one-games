# Keep four, add Sudoku after launch

Status: research memo for the lead. This file does not change `DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md` or `docs/TASKS.md`. Raw notes: `docs/reports/extra-launch-games-notes/`.

**18 Nov 2026.** Do not delay closed test for a 40-game library. Four modes is normal for a daily hub: LinkedIn launched with three, Apple News+ still ships four or five, and Everyday Grids sits at six. Witgrid already holds the two paper-grid Play keywords that actually scale — Sudoku and Nonogram — plus the two LinkedIn-era habits (Crowns, Binary) that clones on Play have not captured. The one cheap high-value extra is **Sudoku 9×9**; a second cheap extra is **Futoshiki or Skyscrapers**. **Path** captures LinkedIn Zip demand and Flow Free’s install mass, but it is not cheap: uniqueness is a second solver, and this codebase has no path Move. Colour sort, number merge, block puzzle, and arrows are not “minimal effort”; they need new input, lives, and they fail the unique no-guess bank. Closed test now waits on Play billing, a 14-day tester run, and human cards — not on missing modes. Adding even the cheap pair now slips that window by about **3–5 weeks**.

## Sudoku 9x9 is cheap only as a copied lane, not a constant

Witgrid’s hard rules do not change for extras: every bank puzzle has **exactly one solution**, the technique solver finishes with **no guess**, puzzle math is **integer-only**, RNG is project **SplitMix64**, play is **offline-first**, and product strings must not use the banned clone names (Crowns not Queens, Binary not Tango, Path not Zip, Calcudoku not KenKen, Nonogram not Picross). The launch bank already uses complete-then-punch plus a brute solution counter, the same method Tatham calls unable to fail once a filled grid exists ([Tatham writing.md](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/devel/writing.html)). Sudoku 9×9 is **Easy** on that generator: same punch loop as Mini Sudoku 6×6, a public technique table (HoDoKu SSTS: singles, locked candidates, subsets, fish, wings), and uniqueness by counting to a second solution ([HoDoKu creating sudokus](https://hodoku.sourceforge.net/en/docs_cre.php); [Final Sudoku generator](https://finalsudoku.com/sudoku-generator)). Futoshiki and Skyscrapers are the same Latin-square family: fill, write all true inequalities or visibility clues, punch while unique. Tatham already ships them as Unequal and Towers under MIT ([Tatham collection](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/)). Killer is a step up because cage partition and the 45-rule sit on top of 9×9; Calcudoku adds four operators and must keep ÷ on integer quotients ([Wikipedia: Killer sudoku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killer_sudoku); [puzzlemix Calcudoku rules](https://www.puzzlemix.com/rules-calcudoku.php)).

The engineering trap is in this repo, not in Sudoku theory. Mini Sudoku is **hardcoded to 6×6**: size, digits `1..6`, 2×3 boxes, six-bit notes, and a keypad that rejects digits above 6. `Move` is a sealed three-variant type (`SetMark`, `SetDigit`, `SetNotes`). Sudoku 9×9 is therefore a **copied lane**, not a one-line constant change. Guru Games hit the same wall: encoding 3×3 boxes into a classic engine was fine, then Killer cages “broke half of what we had written” ([What Building Six Sudoku Variants Taught Us](https://medium.com/@gurugames/what-building-six-sudoku-variants-taught-us-about-software-architecture-f98bf26b901b)). Honest card counts in this codebase: Sudoku 9×9 **7–9 cards / ~2 weeks**; Futoshiki or Skyscrapers **9–11 / 2–3 weeks**; Killer **10–12 / ~3 weeks**; Calcudoku **11–13**. Home, Game, daily chips, endless levels, ads, and hints already iterate `GameRegistry` and **never state a game count**, so shell UI is cheap. Engines are not. Bankgen still hardcodes a `when` on the four launch IDs; a fifth ID currently throws. Each extra still needs **4×150 + 365** unique no-guess puzzles, an H1 add of **80** hand solves, and a U8 reshoot.

## Path captures Zip demand, but Hashi and Loop need new geometry

Nikoli, Conceptis, PuzzleMix, and Tatham all ship the same families Witgrid already chose: Latin squares, shading, loops/paths, object placement, region division ([Wikipedia: Nikoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikoli_(publisher)); [Conceptis Mobile](https://www.conceptispuzzles.com/index.aspx?uri=mobile); [puzzlemix.com](https://puzzlemix.com/); [Tatham collection](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/)). LinkedIn’s 2024–2026 unpaid demand beyond the four launch modes is Path (Zip, 18 Mar 2025), Rectangles (Patches, 18 Mar 2026), then word and merge dailies — not Hashi, Loop, or Nurikabe ([AWA: New LinkedIn Game](https://awa.games/linkedin-games/resources/new-linkedin-game/); [LinkedIn Help: Zip](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a7445030)). NYT’s only original grid-logic add in this window is Pips (dominoes, 18 Aug 2025), not a new Nikoli deep cut ([Wikipedia: The New York Times Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_New_York_Times_Games)). Reddit already points Path fans at Flow Free ([r/puzzles: LinkedIn Games alternatives](https://www.reddit.com/r/puzzles/comments/1gqmcwb/linkedin_games/)).

That demand is real and it is expensive here. Flow-style Path **relaxes uniqueness**; classic Numberlink techniques that assume one solution “cannot be applied” to fill-all Flow ([Any Puzzle Media Numberlink](https://anypuzzle.com/Numberlink)). Ahle’s generator solves **270** Janko puzzles in **0.14 seconds** and still **cannot check uniqueness**; the license is AGPL-3.0, so it is study-only ([thomasahle/numberlink](https://github.com/thomasahle/numberlink)). Witgrid’s own plan already parks Path because the generator needs walls for a unique solution. `Mark` is only EMPTY/FILLED/CROSSED; a multi-colour path does not fit. Hashi and Loop are worse on the canvas: `BoardCanvas` hit-tests **cells**, not edges between islands or lattice lines. Adding a fourth `Move` variant forces every existing engine `when` to change. Slitherlink uniqueness at guess-depth is slow in published generators (**174 s** at depth 1 in one student solver; Appelbe’s 10×10 is **10–30 s** per puzzle), and Witgrid must **reject** any board that needs bifurcation ([davidjosepha/slitherlink](https://github.com/davidjosepha/slitherlink); [Appelbe 2020](https://liamappelbe.medium.com/how-to-generate-slither-link-puzzles-6c65510b2ba1); [timhutton/slinker](https://github.com/timhutton/slinker/blob/main/README.txt)). Tents is the cheap-ish object-placement extra (`SetMark` like Crowns), but Tatham’s own source says generation is hard enough that brute force is the option because clues cannot be thinned ([tents.c comments](https://git.tartarus.org/?p=simon/puzzles.git;a=blob;f=tents.c;hb=HEAD)). Score Path as a **post-launch wave**, not a closed-test extra. Score Bridges and Loop the same: paper-logic, new geometry, 12–15 cards.

## Sort, merge, block, and arrows fail the unique no-guess bank

Lessmore’s Arrows – Puzzle Escape is a 2025–2026 breakout: Google Play “over 100 million” downloads, **4.5** stars on **2.19 million** reviews as of 18 Oct 2026 ([Fantagiochi](https://www.fantagiochi.it/arrows-puzzle-escape-soluzioni/)). LinkedIn added Sumbreak, a neighbour-merge daily, on **18 Nov 2026** ([AWA Sumbreak](https://awa.games/linkedin-games/sumbreak/howto/)). Colour-sort commercial listings sell “500 levels plus infinite play,” not unique banks ([Puzzlette](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.falanapp.puzzlette&hl=en_US)). Those numbers measure a different product. Tatham’s fairness rule is the same as Witgrid’s: a player may fail only through lack of skill; hidden information that still requires an arbitrary choice is unfair ([Tatham writing.md, ch. 6.1](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/devel/writing.html)). Colour sort has many pour sequences; hints are “pour A onto B,” not a named technique. Number merge and 8×8 block-place are endless procedural loops with revive ads; Chaoschemy’s tools for those genres are move advisors, not uniqueness generators ([Chaoschemy tools](https://chaoschemy.com/tools/)). Classic Minesweeper no-guess is **exceptional**, not the Windows default; solubility depends on the first click, so it is a poor static gzip bank unless first-click is stored ([Tatham mines-solver](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/quasiblog/mines-solver/); [minesweeper.now FAQ](https://minesweeper.now/faq/no-guessing-minesweeper)). Klondike fails the same fairness test even with a winnability oracle: Draw-1 thoughtful deals are **90.480%** winnable, which is not a unique no-guess path ([Blake & Gent, arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.12314)).

On the app side those genres also miss “minimal effort.” Colour sort is not a square grid, so `BoardCanvas` does not apply. Arrows need a rotate or slide gesture that `BoardGestures` does not have. Block puzzle needs piece-drag onto an 8×8 field plus a revive card. Lives games reopen PlayController fail policy (plan 8.4) and collide with a done monetisation lane. Card counts sit at **12–16** each, 3–4+ weeks, plus Remote Config keys at game-over instead of `levelsPerInterstitial`. The plan already bans screw sort, hexa sort, tile match, mahjong, jigsaw, five-letter guess, and sliding-block escape; keep sort/merge/block/arrows in that same “different product” bucket until income, not until closed test.

## Closed test waits on Play billing, not on missing modes

On 18 Nov 2026 the remaining *code* cards are **M6** (blocked on the Play product and a licence-tester buy), **M7** analytics, and **S3** release config. Human gates are **H0.1**, **H1** (320 bank solves still open), **H4**, and **H5** (12+ testers for **14** continuous days, then production access). Extra games do not unblock M6. They add surface for the exit check “no puzzle with two solutions,” **+80** H1 solves each, a six-board U8 matrix, and a later start to H5. Two extras now, even Sudoku 9×9 plus Futoshiki, are about **16–20** cards on the critical path x2 (H) → x3 → bank → hand-test → screenshots. That misses the current Phase 5 window by **3–5 calendar weeks**. A generator defect after H5 starts resets the 14-day clock. Wave 1 in the written plan already sits in Phase 6, and the 90-day review adds those games only if income trends toward **$100/month**. After launch, each extra is a store update and a reason to post. Home already scales to any list length; the bottleneck remains guess-free unique generation and, for Path/Bridges/Loop, a new Move or canvas.

## Conclusion

List Witgrid as a **deep four** — unique no-guess banks, offline, Sudoku and Nonogram in the searchable names — not as an unfinished museum next to “40 games.” Crowns and Binary will not win Play search; they win as unlimited fair dailies for people who already have the habit. The first extra that both matches Play demand and fits this engine is Sudoku 9×9 as its own package, copied from Mini Sudoku, with 3×3 boxes and a larger technique table so Expert stays guess-free. Put Futoshiki or Skyscrapers second, Path third once a uniqueness counter and a path Move exist, and leave sort/merge/block/arrows out of the logic-bank roadmap. Ship closed test on the four boards that already have **3,864** validated puzzles.
